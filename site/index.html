Questions answered

Before you press record.

Is what I type recorded?+

No. StepDoc records only the action—for example, “Fill in the Email field.” It never includes the entered value in step metadata.

Where do screenshots live?+

In the Storage bucket of the Supabase project you configure. The extension talks to your StepDoc API; Supabase credentials remain server-side.

Can I deploy the whole app publicly?+

Do not. It is a single-user internal tool without authentication. Expose only explicitly published read-only guide routes, ideally behind network controls appropriate to your environment.

Do exports have branding or limits?+

No mandatory watermark, seat count, or export paywall. Markdown, standalone HTML, PDF, rich text, and embed output are part of the self-hosted project.

Does the simulation capture my screen?+

No. It changes only canned elements already on this page and makes no permission request.
